Every figure carries its tool. Figures from different tools measure different things and are not comparable to one another.

One tool says traffic went up twelvefold. Google says the site got 276 clicks.

All four data sources are live for the first time. Three of them agree that very little changed in September. The fourth, Ahrefs, shows a jump that turns out to be a measurement artefact, and understanding why is the most useful thing in this report.

The link attack escalated sharply in September and now needs an urgent response. Sites linking in went from 567 to 1,111 in one month. Spam links that actually pass value went from 31 to 359. Ninety-one new link-farm domains appeared on a single day, 28 September. Section 09 has the evidence and the recommended order of work.
